# Character bible

Developer notes on the cast: who they are, how they speak, who they know, and what they do in the story.

Create individual sheets only for named characters. Generic entities such as wanderers, guards, and monsters do not need their own sheets.

Character pages distinguish **established content**, **interpretation**, and **proposed development**. Interpretation explains a reading of existing material; proposals remain undecided until adopted. Dialogue and gameplay definitions stay in `game-project/`; pages link to those sources rather than duplicate the full scripts. Update the relevant page when changing a character's story or relationships.
