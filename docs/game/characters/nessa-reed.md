# Nessa Reed

## Overview

Nessa Reed is the fishing tutor beside the pond north of Willowbrook’s fountain. She explains how to equip a rod, fish, and make room for catches, then directs players to Mara to sell their fish. Her introduction is playful, recalling catches that included boots and an offended duck.
