# Mara

## Overview

Mara runs Mara’s Trail Supplies northwest of Willowbrook’s square. She sells gathering tools and boots, buys logs and raw fish, and connects early gathering activities to trading. Her dialogue says she kept the accounts while Rowan supplied timber, Bram supplied iron, and Pip supplied lunch.
