# Captain Ash

## Overview

Captain Ash is the melee combat tutor at the southern edge of Eastwatch’s training yard. He introduces newcomers to fighting with a sword and encourages them to start with the small field rats. His opening advice is brisk and practical: equip the weapon before making threats.
