# Pip

## Overview

Pip is the innkeeper at the Copper Kettle southwest of Willowbrook’s square. He sells bread, apples, and health potions, offers a welcoming joke about muddy adventurers, and shares rumors connecting Old Fen, Orla’s expedition notes, and the shrine in Willow Wood.
