# Bains

## Overview

Bains is the Eastwatch lookout, stationed near the road beyond the training yard. He warns newcomers about quarry rats and the armed outlaws occupying the abandoned watchhouse, recommends starting with the weaker field rats, and gives directions back to Willowbrook.
