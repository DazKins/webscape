# DazKins

## Overview

DazKins is the developer’s characterisation of himself within Webscape. He is a core, central protagonist who leads much of the early game and remains involved across several quests. He is intended to be the first character the player speaks to, establishing a familiar presence they will return to as their adventure develops.

## Personality and voice

Confident and assertive. DazKins takes the initiative, speaks directly, and gives the player a clear sense of purpose. His dialogue should convey someone comfortable taking the lead and acting on his decisions.

## Relationships

**Player:** Their first contact and a recurring early-game lead. Their connection develops over several quests, making DazKins a familiar central figure throughout the opening adventure.

**Other characters:** __not specified__

## Story and behavior

**Intended direction:** DazKins introduces the player to the adventure, drives much of its early progression, and takes an active role in several quests. His role extends beyond the initial errand. The details of those additional quests are __not specified__.

**Currently implemented:** He gives First Errand near Willowbrook’s village square: collect a mysterious key from the wayfarer chest in Rowan’s Lodge, then defeat a rat at Eastwatch, earning an Ancient Scroll. He wants to understand the rats’ interest in the key, and Orla says he suspects a connection to the ruined shrine in Willow Wood. His first-contact role and involvement in additional quests are design direction for future game content.
