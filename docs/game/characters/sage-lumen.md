# Sage Lumen

## Overview

Sage Lumen is the magic tutor outside the Lantern Archive in southeast Willowbrook. He explains staff combat and casting, directs players to Orla for magic supplies, and points them toward expedition notes and the western shrine. His scholarly introduction includes a joke about singeing the inn’s curtains.
