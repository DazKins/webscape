# Orla

## Overview

Orla runs Lantern Curios in the Lantern Archive southeast of Willowbrook’s square. She sells magic staves and stones, keeps weathered expedition notes in the archive, and discusses the travelers who built the ruined shrine. She links that mystery to Old Fen’s memories and DazKins’ theory about the key.
