# Rowan Moss

## Overview

Rowan Moss is the woodcutting tutor beside the west road in Willow Wood. He explains axes, logs, and tree regrowth, points players to Mara to sell their timber, and gives directions to the ruined shrine. His lodge holds the wayfarer chest involved in DazKins’ errand; his dialogue presents him as a plain-speaking contributor to Willowbrook’s construction.
